# Learning log

1. **One template, many pages.** The menu, footer, disclaimer and email are written once in `src/build.py` and stamped onto every page, like letterhead. Change it once, every page updates.
2. **The cascade.** When two style rules disagree, the more specific one wins. That is why the gold buttons first came out white.
3. **Images are made, not coded.** Code builds the frame (layout, motion, light). Beautiful pictures come from a camera or an image generator.
